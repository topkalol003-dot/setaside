    Questions

    Who has to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

    Generally, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more in federal tax for the year after withholding and credits, the IRS expects you to pay during the year. That is common for freelancers and 1099 contractors because nobody withholds tax from their pay. See IRS Form 1040-ES.

    What is self-employment tax?

    It is Social Security and Medicare tax for people who work for themselves: 15.3% on 92.35% of net profit, with the Social Security part capped at the yearly wage base ($184,500 for 2026, $192,300 for 2027). It applies once your net profit reaches $400.

    What is the safe harbor?

    You generally avoid an underpayment penalty if you pay at least 90% of this year's tax, or 100% of last year's tax (110% if last year's AGI was over $150,000), spread across the four due dates.

    Does this include state tax?

    Only as a simple percentage you enter under "More options". State rules vary a lot, so check your state's estimated tax page.

    Do you store my numbers?

    No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
